Milin Creator Studio

A free desktop app for short-video creators. It runs on your own computer and helps you:

  • cut raw footage into short vertical clips (auto-trim silences and retakes, subtitles, text effects)
  • keep a library of your products, pictures and reference clips
  • plan a posting schedule that avoids repeating the same video or product
  • send a finished clip to your own TikTok account as a draft at the time you planned — you review it, add details and publish it yourself in TikTok
